Presenter Mode

Clamor turns student group chats into source-backed operational memory.

Sleeping Giant

GroupMe is chat without memory

Student groups coordinate there, but decisions, deadlines, files, and tasks disappear into scrollback.

After

AI becomes the memory layer

Digest, Ask, Actions, and Dashboard convert scrollback into decisions, owners, blockers, and proof.

Vision

Start narrow, expand later

Win campus GroupMe-style communities first, then connect the other tools those communities already use.

Business

Local first, paid AI second

Most alerts and stats run locally. Heavier AI answers, reports, imports, and cross-platform memory become paid upgrades.

Demo Script

  1. Show the busy chat and channel structure.
  2. Say: "GroupMe makes you scroll. Clamor gives memory."
  3. Open Digest to reduce noisy messages into decisions, blockers, and tasks.
  4. Ask Clamor: "What decisions were made about funding?"
  5. Point to source cards to prove trust and explainability.
  6. Open Dashboard to show saved time, signal rate, and low AI cost.
