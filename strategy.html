Business Strategy

Affordable enough for clubs. Valuable enough for campuses.

Clamor keeps basic intelligence cheap by running most detection locally, then monetizes heavier AI features that leaders and campuses will pay for: source-backed answers, reports, imports, and cross-platform memory.

1

Beachhead

Student clubs, Greek life, dorms, hackathon teams, project teams, and campus programs.

2

Buyer

Club leaders start it. Campus departments, councils, and programs can pay for bundled access.

3

Reason To Pay

Less leadership burnout, fewer missed deadlines, better onboarding, and institutional memory that survives turnover.

Revenue Model

  • Free: messaging, local alerts, basic summaries, and activity stats.
  • Club Plus: deeper AI answers, weekly reports, imports, dashboards, and source-backed history.
  • Campus Plan: bulk access for departments managing many student communities.

Cost Control

  • Do not call paid AI on every message.
  • Use local logic for tags, alerts, counts, routing, and simple summaries.
  • Use Groq/OpenAI only for heavier Ask, Digest, reports, and cross-platform memory.
  • Cache repeated answers and limit heavy usage by plan.

Growth Loop

  • One leader invites the club.
  • Members rely on summaries and alerts.
  • New officers inherit the memory, so the product survives turnover.
  • Successful groups pull in partner clubs and campus offices.
Judge Objection Defense

Answer the scary questions before they hurt you.

Why not Slack?

Slack proves demand for AI workplace memory. Clamor targets low-budget, high-churn communities that do not operate like enterprise teams.

Why not GroupMe copying this?

GroupMe can add AI features, but Clamor is built around the workflow: decisions, owners, alerts, source cards, turnover, and imports.

Would students pay?

Some clubs will not. That is why the best paid motion is Club Plus for leaders and Campus Plans for departments with real budgets.

What about privacy?

Clamor uses source-backed answers, workspace permissions, local-first processing, and limited paid AI calls for sensitive context.

What Not To Do
Do not say "we beat Slack."

Say Slack is enterprise. Clamor is campus and community native.

Do not say "we replace Discord."

Say Clamor starts with GroupMe-style coordination and expands later.

Do not lead with Watchlist.

Lead with Digest, Ask, source cards, actions, and dashboard.

Do not overbuild the pitch.

The winning idea is simple: Clamor turns buried chat into decisions, tasks, alerts, and answers.
